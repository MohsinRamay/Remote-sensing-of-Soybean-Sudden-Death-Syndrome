# Marsden disease trajectories and symptom timing by season

Extend the [2016 disease protocol](../phase2/PROTOCOL.md) to 2017 and 2018, then rerun 2016 on the same definitions for comparison. Use every disease-rated quadrat in each season. Confirm the 2017–2018 disease plot assignments against the archived field diagrams. The separately corrected reflectance records play no role in this cohort.

## Severity trajectories and block comparisons

At each recorded assessment, average quadrats within a cropping-system main plot and give the four blocks equal weight. Estimate the severity trajectory by cropping system, paired contrasts within blocks, final recorded severity, and time-weighted mean severity over each year's observed window. The time-weighted mean is trapezoidal AUDPC divided by the number of days between the first and final assessments. Annual windows differ in timing and length.

Repeat the 2016 conditional whole-curve and AUDPC permutation checks using all 1,296 assignments of the three complete plot profiles within each of four blocks. The uniform assignment calculation is conditional on exchangeability of the observed soybean plots under the sharp null. This assumption includes the sampled outcome profiles. The original assignment record and reasons for smaller 2018 samples remain relevant. Use these tail areas as exploratory summaries. For contrasts, report pointwise 95% t intervals across paired block differences. In 2018, compare the four-block point estimates with those from the three blocks with 10 rated quadrats per plot.

## Recorded symptom timing and progression

Use the first positive severity rating to construct the interval from the last below-threshold visit to first detection. Mark first-visit positives as left-censored and records that stay below threshold as right-censored. Use incidence above zero and severity of at least 5% as sensitivity endpoints. Compare cumulative detection on the actual dates. First detection measures when symptoms were recorded.

At the final assessment, decompose each paired severity difference into a component associated with the fraction ever detected and one associated with mean severity among detected quadrats. Within a plot, mean severity is exactly the fraction ever detected multiplied by their mean current severity. For each paired contrast, split the product difference using the two groups' midpoint values of each factor, then average the components across blocks. Use only block pairs with at least one detected quadrat in both plots, and report their count. Keep subsequent zeros in the detected group's mean. This is a descriptive decomposition. Severity among detected quadrats depends on time since detection and which quadrats entered that group.

For a separate progression description, use the first positive severity rating and its next recorded visit among quadrats detected after the first visit and before the last one. Report the observed change and elapsed days, with equal plot weights within rotation, and the number of eligible quadrats and plots. The qualifying populations and time since biological onset may differ. Treat this as description of subsequent severity in the eligible quadrats.
