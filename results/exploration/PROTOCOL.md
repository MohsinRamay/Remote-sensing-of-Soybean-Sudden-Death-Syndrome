# Closing analyses for the SDS exploration

The new comparisons are exploratory extensions of the question families and endpoints in the research plan. Treatments are compared at their assignment level. Marsden quadrats are subsamples of 12 soybean main plots per season in four blocks. Hinds has 60 microplots in six blocks. Each model comparison uses the same eligible rows within its stated cohort.

| Question | Model or check | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Equal-weight plot-visit severity. Fractional-logit visit factor and three-column natural spline, with rotation interactions. A bounded logistic growth curve fitted separately by rotation supplies a nonlinear comparator. | Whole-block holdout. The existing conditional permutation test supplies the whole-curve comparison. |
| Q2 | First recorded positive severity with left-, interval- and right-censoring. Onset-aligned summaries by days from first recorded detection, excluding first-visit positives. | Observed dates and contributing plot counts. Alternative incidence and 5% severity thresholds remain in the annual disease analysis. |
| Q3 | Severity FPCA on daily linear interpolation of observed years, centered within plot and weighted to give each plot equal influence. Compare with conventional summary scores and count same-plot pairs within 1 point of time-weighted mean severity but at least 10 points apart at the final visit. | Leave one block out to test loading and two-dimensional subspace stability. Pair counts share quadrats and are descriptive. |
| Q4 | Extend the two-band association to Hinds. Pair each disease record with the latest same-day or prior complete acquisition within four days and omit the disputed acquisitions. Compare pooled and plot-plus-visit slopes by block, alternate severity, strict prior dates, omission of the last visit and omission of the disputed date. | Equal-weight block slopes. Marsden uses within-quadrat and plot-by-visit adjustment under its own design. |
| Q5 | Joint severity-incidence FPCA within Marsden plots after equalizing total channel variation. Compare pointwise and AUDPC correlations. At Hinds, compare percentage versions, reconstruct incidence with each stand denominator and report block- and treatment-adjusted correlations by date. | Leave-one-block-out loading stability at Marsden. Quantify Hinds mismatches where counts exist. Keep separate the recorded percentages and the candidate denominators. |
| Q6 | Treatment-only regression against final severity, trapezoidal average severity (scaled AUDPC), recorded onset, two training-derived severity PC scores and combined models. Use unit ridge on standardized disease inputs and unpenalized treatment indicators. | Hold out whole blocks, give each plot equal weight in training and scoring, and compare Hinds severity versions. Evaluate retrospective yield prediction using full-season disease history. |
| Q7 | Use paired 660/810 nm acquisitions from the 21 days strictly before each landmark with a latest pair no older than seven days. Add linear slopes and then quadratic basis coefficients from each observed prefix to the latest-spectrum model. Refit all baselines and spectral models within each history denominator. | Compare the two-acquisition and three-acquisition cohorts separately. Keep the same four folds and all three endpoints. |
| Q8 | Compare treatment differences over the overlapping calendar window using linear interpolation. For prediction, leave out a target year and the same physical block in both source years. Compare cropping-system risk, calendar date plus cropping system, and the addition of latest spectrum. | Slides 2–4 of the manifest-verified `Marsden Schemes.pptx` and the numbered 2017 layout corroborate block positions. Evaluate each held-out block using Brier score, within-block AUROC, average precision, mean predicted risk and sensitivity at a 20% opportunity budget with fractional ties. Flag target landmarks outside the training calendar range. |

For Q7, fit the fold-specific standardized spectral terms with the fixed-offset ridge model from the latest-spectrum comparison. The polynomial terms are a low-rank representation of the sampled prefix. They depend on observed dates only. For Q8, use the smoothed cropping-system risk on the training years as the offset and day relative to August 1 on a common calendar. Fit calendar and spectral terms jointly for the augmented model. The target is a new block and year at the same farm. These records support no independent out-of-site test.

The analysis ledger and output index are in the [exploration report](REPORT.md). All observation-level derived outputs are written under an ignored `local/` directory. Archive and repository sources undergo manifest hash checks where the scripts read them directly.
